The rules

  • Only for profiles you passed on (a Strikeout or mixed decisions).
  • A standard retry needs a 7-day wait, with a maximum of 2 retries per profile.
  • You'll see different photos where possible.
  • Premium can skip the wait only. It still counts toward the 2-retry limit.
  • A retry never overrides a rejection, block, report, or anyone's consent. Some profiles simply aren't available, for reasons we keep private.

Safety and reporting

Your safety comes first.

Your controls

  • Unmatch ends a conversation.
  • Block hides the profile from you and removes it from Second Chance.
  • Report flags harassment, fakes, scams or anything unsafe. Find all three in any conversation (⋯), on a profile, or under the card in Discover.

How reporting works

In this prototype, reports are saved in this browser only. No moderator receives them. A real STRIKEOUT would route reports to a trained review team (planned for V9) and a reported profile could never be pushed back to you through retries.

Dating safely

  • Meet in a public place and tell a friend where you'll be.
  • Do a video call before meeting.
  • Never send money or share financial details, however good the story.
  • Keep chatting in the app until you're comfortable.
  • Trust your instincts, and leave if something feels off.

If you need help

In an emergency in Canada, call 911. To report fraud, contact the Canadian Anti-Fraud Centre (antifraudcentre-centreantifraude.ca). General safety information, not legal advice.
